Learn AI Observability with Phoebe

Two thirds of the run was inside no span at all

Tuesday, 09:14. An analytics bot starts sending executives stale revenue numbers, intermittently. No error is raised. No alert fires. The p95 latency dashboard is green and the answers read as confidently as ever. The failing step is not slow, not broken and not lying - it is simply not instrumented, so as far as the trace is concerned it cannot fail at all. This course is about the trace: spans and the OpenTelemetry GenAI conventions with their real current attribute names, instrumenting an agent whose loop is width rather than depth, per-span cost and latency that survive that loop, the failure taxonomy that maps every mode to the one signal that reveals it, sampling that keeps the slow traces instead of a random 1%, burn-rate alerting instead of thresholds that cry wolf, and the privacy split that lets you debug without hoarding your users' prompts. At its centre, a live trace explorer: switch instrumentation coverage on one lever at a time and watch the visible wall clock climb from 32.4% to 100% while the diagnosis goes from zero of five questions answerable to five - then switch on full content capture and watch the diagnosis refuse to move while retention jumps ten and a half times.
